## 1. Executive Summary & Value Proposition

KIRA is not "a fintech app with an AI feature." It is a regulated money-movement platform where the AI is the interface and the deterministic ledger is the authority. That distinction is the entire engineering problem, and it is the problem I have spent my career on: building systems where an incorrect balance is not a bug ticket but a regulatory event.

**The one-line thesis I would bring to the role:** *AI may decide what the user probably means. It may never decide what the user's money is.* Everything below is the engineering that enforces that sentence.

**Specific alignment to Bjak's stack and domain constraints:**
- **ASEAN fragmentation:** multi-jurisdiction licensing (SC Malaysia, OJK/IDX, MAS, SEC Philippines/Thailand), per-market ticker universes, per-market trading calendars, per-market tax/withholding logic. This is a configuration-and-policy problem, not a fork-per-country problem.
- **Regulated rails as hard dependencies:** broker-dealer partners, clearing/settlement, custody, FX, and payment channels all fail in ways you cannot retry blindly. The architecture must treat every external call as an at-least-once, non-idempotent-by-default hazard.
- **Superapp surface area:** payments, remittance, insurance, wealth, and trading sharing one identity, one balance abstraction, one notification model, and one audit spine — with segregation-of-funds enforced at the ledger level, not the UI level.
- **Mobile-first, unreliable-network reality:** users on 3G in tier-2 cities will double-tap "Buy." The product must be correct under duplicate submission, and honest about state ("submitted, not yet filled") rather than optimistic.

**Plane 1 — Advisory (LLM):** converts natural language into a *typed, schema-validated intent document*. It has read access to market data and the user's masked portfolio. It has zero write access to money.

**Plane 2 — Deterministic Core:** owns balances, orders, limits, and the ledger. Only this plane can mutate financial state. Every mutation is a double-entry posting.

**Plane 3 — External:** broker-dealers, custodians, payment rails. Every interaction is wrapped in an adapter with its own idempotency, retry policy, circuit breaker, and reconciliation path.

### 2.2 Data modeling — money is an integer, always
