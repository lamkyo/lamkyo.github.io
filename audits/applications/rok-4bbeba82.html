## 2. Deep Technical Solution Blueprint

### 2.1 Problem framing

A Solutions Architect at ExtraHop is, in practice, asked to build **repeatable extraction of high-value data from arbitrary customer environments**, and to make that extraction *portable* across verticals. The architectural pattern I would bring to that problem is a **three-plane architecture**:

1. **Acquisition Plane** — captures, decodes, and normalizes wire data with a hard guarantee about *what was lost*.
2. **Reasoning Plane** — stateful, windowed analysis (Trigger Script runtime, KPI engine, detection evaluation).
3. **Delivery Plane** — API, dashboards, exports, ticketing/SIEM egress.
