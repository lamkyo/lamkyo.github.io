## 1. Executive Summary & Value Proposition

I am applying for the Senior Backend Developer position because it combines three areas in which strong systems engineering is especially valuable:

1. **TypeScript backend engineering:** production Node.js and NestJS services, REST and GraphQL APIs, asynchronous processing, and maintainable domain architecture.
2. **Reliability at business-critical boundaries:** payment initiation, balance accounting, authorization, third-party integrations, auditability, and failure recovery.
3. **Cloud-native delivery:** PostgreSQL, Redis, message-driven workflows, Docker, CI/CD, observability, automated testing, and zero-downtime deployments.

Proxify AB appears to operate a **remote talent marketplace** where the platform must coordinate candidate discovery, engagement matching, client workflows, contractor availability, and predictable payment operations. That domain places unusually strong requirements on backend correctness. A minor defect in a recommendation feature is inconvenient; a double withdrawal, duplicated payout, inconsistent balance, or authorization bypass is a direct commercial and trust failure.

My approach would be to combine:

- A **well-structured NestJS modular monolith** for transactional business capabilities.
- Explicit **domain boundaries** that can later be extracted into services only when scale or team ownership justifies it.
- **PostgreSQL as the authoritative transactional store**.
- An **outbox-based event workflow** for asynchronous matching, notifications, analytics, and payout processing.
- **Idempotency, immutable financial ledger entries, optimistic or pessimistic concurrency controls**, and auditable state transitions.
- Defense-in-depth through OIDC/OAuth 2.1, short-lived tokens, RBAC/ABAC, encryption, rate limiting, and sensitive-data minimization.
- Production evidence through OpenTelemetry, Prometheus, structured logs, SLOs, load testing, and deployment automation.
