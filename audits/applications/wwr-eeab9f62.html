## 1. Executive Summary & Value Proposition

### The thesis

Lithic's assessment function has three properties that make it an engineering problem in disguise:

1. **It is a decision system with a reproducibility requirement.** A client risk tier, a reserve number, or a vendor control-effectiveness rating must be derivable again, from the same inputs, two years later, in front of a regulator. That is a versioned, deterministic scoring engine with an immutable input log — not a spreadsheet.
2. **It is an evidence pipeline under adversarial audit.** Partner banks and examiners do not ask "what is the tier?" They ask "show me the artifact, the date you received it, who reviewed it, what you concluded, and what changed since." That is content-addressed storage, provenance, and bitemporal history.
3. **It is explicitly AI-forward, and the role owns the build.** "Test, deploy, and tune agentic tooling across the assessment workflows... document the context that makes the output repeatable and defensible." This is exactly model-gated automation: structured extraction, schema validation, confidence thresholds, abstention, mandatory human decision on every rating and limit, and an eval harness that proves the automation did not degrade the judgment.

The value proposition: **you get a risk owner who also owns the platform.** Most risk associates hand requirements to engineering and wait a quarter. Most engineers cannot write a defensible residual-risk position. This role needs both, and the agentic tooling mandate is precisely where the two skill sets stop being separable.

## 2. Deep Technical Solution Blueprint

### 2.1 Architectural stance

Three principles drive every decision:

- **The register is the system of record. Nothing else is.** No parallel spreadsheet. If a vendor tier, a control rating, or a reserve number exists in two places, the examiner will find the disagreement.
- **Human judgment is a first-class, recorded object — not an exception path.** Every rating has an `origin` (`rubric_engine`, `analyst_override`, `imported_legacy`) and a reasoning trail. Automation produces *candidates*; humans produce *decisions*.
- **Defensibility is a storage property, not a documentation habit.** Repro­ducibility, provenance, and tamper-evidence are enforced by the schema, not by discipline.
