## 1. Executive Summary & Value Proposition

Federal data governance fails in the seam between **policy** and **platform**. Agencies do not lack governance frameworks — they lack the plumbing that makes a catalog, a lineage graph, and a stewardship workflow survive an ATO, a FedRAMP boundary, and a multi-cloud estate that grew by accretion across two decades of contract vehicles.

That seam is exactly where I work.

My background sits at the intersection the JD describes, but in an order most candidates get backwards:

**Why this maps to Collibra specifically.** Collibra's value proposition in the public sector is not the catalog — it is *time-to-first-value inside a compliance envelope*. The architecture that determines whether an agency renews is:

1. **Edge/agent connectivity** across ATO boundaries with no inbound firewall exceptions.
2. **Idempotent, resumable metadata ingestion** at 10⁷–10⁸ asset scale without duplicating stewardship work.
3. **Deterministic lineage** that auditors can trust, and that does not silently truncate at depth 4.
4. **Governance-as-code** — the ability to promote operating models across dev/stage/prod enclaves exactly like application config.

My proposal below treats all four as engineering problems with measurable SLOs, not as configuration tasks.

**Commercial alignment.** The posted band ($96K–$120K base + commission) is understood and accepted as a starting frame; my interest is the public-sector motion itself — account expansion, repeatable delivery artifacts, and utilization discipline.
