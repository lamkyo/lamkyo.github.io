# Engineering Application: Senior Full-Stack Developer - Marketplace Web & Mobile Platform at Adventure Travel 365 --- ## 1. Executive Summary & Value Proposition I build two-sided marketplaces end-to-end — and the specific failure modes that kill contractor-referral platforms (cold-start liquidity, lead-quality disputes, payment trust, spam inquiries) are problems I've architected against in production, not theoretical ones. **Why this matches Adventure Travel 365's problem space:** - **Domain adjacency:** Thumbtack/Angi-class platforms are fundamentally *lead-delivery systems with payment trust layers*. I've built the equivalent core loops: service request → matching/ranking → provider attribution → escrowed payment → review, with the operational tooling (admin moderation, dispute handling, payout reconciliation) that turns a demo into a business. - **Two-sided by design, not by accident:** The hard part of your platform isn't the CRUD — it's *supply-side quality*, *request intent capture*, and *conversion attribution*. My proposals below are structured around those, because they determine whether contractors actually pay for leads. - **Full lifecycle ownership:** I take projects through architecture → build → hardening → launch → iteration, and I communicate in product outcomes, not framework jargon, for non-technical stakeholders. That's exactly the "big picture + ask the right questions" profile you described. - **AI where it earns its keep:** AI-assisted development in my day-to-day workflow (spec-driven codegen, review, test synthesis), plus in-product AI features that are cheap to run and genuinely useful here: request-description → structured lead categorization, provider-match ranking, review-signal summarization, and admin anomaly detection. **Recommended stack (and why):** **Next.js (TypeScript) + React Native/Expo** on the front, **NestJS (TypeScript) modular monolith** on the back, **PostgreSQL + PostGIS + Redis**, **Stripe Connect** for marketplace payments, **AWS (ECS Fargate)** or **Cloudflare/Vercel-adjacent** hosting. One language across web/mobile/API minimizes context-switching for a small team; a modular monolith scales further than people assume and avoids premature microservice tax; Postgres gives you transactions, JSONB flexibility, and full-text/geo search in one operational surface.
