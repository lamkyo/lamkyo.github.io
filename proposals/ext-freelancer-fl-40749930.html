Executive Technical Hook **The core pain point:** multi-domain transactional mail on dedicated IPv4s fails at the *reputation and authentication layer*, not the SMTP layer. Anyone can get Postfix to accept and relay mail. The hard problems are: - **Per-IP, per-domain sender reputation isolation** — each dedicated IPv4 must present consistent HELO, PTR, and DKIM alignment for its assigned domains, or you burn IP reputation across the whole pool. - **DMARC alignment under multi-domain load** — SPF must be evaluated against the correct envelope sender, and DKIM must sign with the domain matching the `From:` header, not the relay host. - **Rspamd scoring tuned for transactional traffic** — default rules over-penalize legitimate bulk senders (missing `List-Unsubscribe`, HTML-heavy templates), causing false positives that are invisible until your users report missing mail. My proposal treats these as first-class engineering requirements, not afterthoughts. --- ## 2.
