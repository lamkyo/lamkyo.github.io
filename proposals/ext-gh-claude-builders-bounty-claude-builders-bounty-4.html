2. **Dependencies** – `requests` (GitHub API), `anthropic` (Claude client), `click` (CLI ergonomics), `pydantic` (typed config), `pytest` + `responses` (mock HTTP).  
3. **Design pattern** – a single `ClaudeReviewer` class that encapsulates:
