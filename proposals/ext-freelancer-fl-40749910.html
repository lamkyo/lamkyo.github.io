Executive Technical Hook A wholesale order site lives or dies on four things, and in your vertical, three of them are compliance-critical: **Tiered price resolution.** A case of beverages at retail price in a wholesale cart is a margin leak. Pricing must resolve per authenticated account — not per session, not per cookie. The catalog renders the same, the price matrix differs. **A private order sheet that is genuinely private.** Authenticated only, `noindex`, cache-bypassed, and gated behind approval — no public registration. An unapproved signup must never see a single SKU or price. **Age and license gating on tobacco and vapes.** This is where most freelancers get you into trouble. Server-side enforcement, unbypassable by client manipulation, with an audit trail of every age affirmation (timestamp, IP, account) so you can evidence due diligence. Age restricted categories are quarantined from public crawling and from guest carts entirely. **Reordering, not browsing.** Your buyers aren't shopping — they're replenishing. Favorites lists, SKU search by item number, "reorder last invoice," and CSV order export are what turn this from a website into a tool your accounts actually use. Everything below is engineered around those four realities, and nothing else. --- ## 2.
