Executive Hook — The Diagnostic You don't need another video loop. You need a **deterministic sensor-to-screen state machine** that survives a 12-hour retail day without a single dropped trigger. Here is what most bidders will miss, and what will quietly break your deployment: - **Nexmosphere X-series sensors are not plug-and-play over USB.** They speak a serial protocol over the X-talk / XN interface. If the integrator treats them as HID devices, you get intermittent triggers and no error surface. The correct path is a **serial listener with a framed parser**, not an event listener on a keyboard emulation. - **BrightSign's BrightScript is single-threaded per renderer.** Blocking I/O on the main thread — a synchronous serial read, a slow HTTP call to your CMS — will stall the video decoder and produce visible stutter. The fix is a **worker-thread architecture** with a message queue between the sensor layer and the presentation layer. - **Sensor debounce is not optional.** In a museum or retail floor, a single approach gesture fires 3–8 raw events. Without a debounce window and an edge-triggered state model, your content will flicker and your analytics will be garbage. - **CMS sync must be idempotent and offline-tolerant.** If the venue's Wi-Fi drops, the signage must keep playing the last-known-good manifest and reconcile on reconnect — not blank the screen. This proposal delivers exactly that: a hardened, testable, handover-ready interactive signage stack — not a demo. --- ## 2.
