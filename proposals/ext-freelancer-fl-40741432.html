# Technical Proposal & Cover Letter ## Wearable Device Prototyping on EasyEDA — High-Power LED Strobe Controller --- Dear Client, I reviewed your posting for a compact, high-power LED strobe controller board targeting JLCPCB Economic PCBA. I understand the core challenge: fitting a high-current strobe driver into a wearable form factor while keeping the design clean enough for JLCPCB's SMT assembly line — no hand-soldering, no DFM surprises, no respins. That is exactly the kind of board I build. I am proposing a complete turnkey delivery: schematic capture, PCB layout, BOM finalization, and fabrication/assembly file generation — all in EasyEDA, all production-ready for JLCPCB Economic PCBA. --- ## 1. Executive Technical Hook High-power LED strobes on wearables fail in predictable ways: thermal runaway from undersized copper, voltage sag under strobe load collapsing the MCU, EMI from fast-edge switching radiating into nearby sensors, and — most commonly — boards that work on the bench but get rejected by the assembly house because of footprint mismatches or missing paste layers. My approach eliminates each of these failure modes by design: - **Power integrity first.** The strobe drive stage gets dedicated copper pours, thermal relief patterns, and via stitching sized for peak pulse current — not average current. - **Component selection locked to JLCPCB's Economic PCBA parts library.** Every BOM line is a part JLCPCB stocks and assembles at the Economic tier. No extended-stock substitutions, no "or equivalent" ambiguity. - **DFM/DFT checked before you ever see a Gerber.** I run design-rule checks against JLCPCB's published capabilities (minimum trace/space, annular ring, solder mask clearance, paste mask expansion) so the first spin is the only spin. --- ## 2. Architecture & Technical Plan ### Phase 1 — Schematic Capture & Circuit Design (Day 1–2) - **LED driver topology selection.** Depending on your target LED (e.g., Cree XP-E2, Luxeon Rebel, or similar), I will size the constant-current driver stage — whether that's a dedicated strobe driver IC (e.g., MAX16834, LT3932) or a discrete MOSFET + current-sense topology — for your desired strobe energy (joules per flash) and repetition rate. - **MCU / trigger interface.** If the strobe is triggered by an external signal or onboard MCU, I will include the appropriate input conditioning (level shifting, ESD protection, optoisolation if needed). - **Power input & protection.** Reverse-polarity protection, input TVS diode, and input capacitor bank sized for the strobe's peak current draw without drooping the rail. - **Wearable-specific considerations.** Flex-rigid or rigid-flex if the form factor demands it; otherwise, a rigid board with mounting holes and strain relief for the battery/input connector. **Deliverable:** Complete EasyEDA schematic with annotated net classes, design rules, and a preliminary BOM. ### Phase 2 — PCB Layout & DFM Optimization (Day 3–5) - **Stack-up.** 2-layer (default for Economic PCBA) or 4-layer if signal integrity or EMI demands it. I will recommend the most cost-effective option that meets your electrical requirements. - **Placement strategy.** High-current paths kept short and direct; sensitive analog/signal traces routed away from the strobe loop; thermal vias under LED pads and driver ICs. - **Copper & trace sizing.** All high-current traces calculated for acceptable temperature rise at peak pulse current (using IPC-2221 or empirical derating). No guesswork. - **Silkscreen & assembly aids.** Clear polarity markings, pin-1 indicators, fiducials (global and local for fine-pitch parts), and a clean assembly drawing. - **Design rule check (DRC).** Full DRC pass against JLCPCB design rules before file export. Zero violations. **Deliverable:** Completed PCB layout in EasyEDA with DRC-clean status, 3D preview, and assembly drawing. ### Phase 3 — Manufacturing File Generation & Handover (Day 6–7) - **Gerber generation.** RS-274X Gerbers for all layers (copper, solder mask, silkscreen, paste, board outline, drill). - **Drill files.** NC drill format with proper tool table. - **Pick-and-place (XY) file.** Centroid file for JLCPCB's SMT assembly, with correct rotation and layer assignment. - **BOM finalization.** CSV with JLCPCB part numbers (LCSC), quantities, reference designators, and package codes. Every part verified in stock. - **Assembly drawing.** PDF with component placements, polarity callouts, and any special instructions. - **EasyEDA project handover.** Full project transferred to your EasyEDA account with all source files, libraries, and version history intact. **Deliverable:** Complete JLCPCB PCBA fabrication package — upload-ready. --- ## 3.
